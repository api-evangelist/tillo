Blue Light Card Launches In-App Shopping Cards Powered by Tillo Blue Light Card, the UK's leading rewards service for six million frontline workers, has transformed how members access gift cards. The new in-app Shopping Cards experience, powered by Tillo's embedded platform , eliminates the friction of leaving the app to complete purchases. Members can now browse, purchase, and store digital gift cards seamlessly within the Blue Light Card environment - delivering the speed and simplicity frontline workers deserve.
