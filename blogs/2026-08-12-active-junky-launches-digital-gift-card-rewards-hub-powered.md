Active Junky, the U.S. cashback and shopping rewards platform, has launched a branded digital gift card and rewards hub for its members. Powered by Tillo StoreFront , Tillo's turnkey white label gift card mall, members can now buy discounted digital gift cards, earn cashback on those purchases, and cash out their rewards balance onto gift cards worth more than the balance itself.
