Tillo, the global gift card, rewards and incentives platform, has partnered with HiThrive, an employee recognition and rewards software provider, to help companies scale recognition across distributed workforces. The partnership gives HiThrive customers access to Tillo's full digital rewards catalog through a single API integration , powering personalized employee reward options in more than 100 countries.
