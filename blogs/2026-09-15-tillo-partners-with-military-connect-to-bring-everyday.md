Tillo and Military Connect Partner to Launch Exclusive Military Gift Cards for 30 Million+ Community Members Tillo, the global gift card platform, has announced a strategic partnership with Military Connect to power its new "Military Gift Cards" program. Utilizing Tillo StoreFront technology, the initiative provides verified U.S. service members, veterans, and military families with instant access to discounted digital gift cards from over 100 leading retail, dining, travel, and entertainment brands.
