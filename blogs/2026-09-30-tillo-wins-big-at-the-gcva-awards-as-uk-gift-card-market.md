Tillo wins two GCVA Awards 2026: Best B2C Product or Service and Industry Service Provider Tillo, the global gift card platform, has won two awards at the Gift Card and Voucher Association (GCVA) Awards 2026 : Best B2C Product or Service 2026 , for its supermarket gift card cashback program with Quidco , and Industry Service Provider 2026 . The wins come as the UK gift card and voucher market has its strongest half-year on record, according to the GCVA's H1 2026 Sales Analysis & Outlook (UK) report.
