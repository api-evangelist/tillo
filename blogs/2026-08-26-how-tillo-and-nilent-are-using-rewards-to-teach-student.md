Tillo, the global gift card, rewards and incentives platform, has partnered with NILENT, the gamified education platform for Name, Image and Likeness (NIL), to help student athletes build financial literacy skills through rewards-based learning. Powered by Tillo StoreFront , the partnership pairs short financial education modules with real gift card rewards, giving athletes a practical way to build money habits that carry beyond college.
